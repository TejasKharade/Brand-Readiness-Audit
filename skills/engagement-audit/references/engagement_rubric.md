# On-Site Engagement & Context Retention Rubric

## Key Audit Criteria

1. **Above-the-Fold Clarity**:
   - Is `<h1>` present and clear?
   - Does it summarize the page topic in under 10 words?

2. **Navigation & Breadcrumbs**:
   - Are `<nav>` elements present with semantic structure?
   - Is breadcrumb JSON-LD (`BreadcrumbList`) present?

3. **CTA & Pathing**:
   - Is there at least one clear primary Action/Conversion target (`<a>` or `<button>`)?
